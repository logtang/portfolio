Research

My research is on evaluating visualizations: designing studies that test whether new visualization techniques actually help people understand complex data. At the VIDI Lab I work on the COEQWAL initiative, which builds tools for analyzing California water policy.

Interests

Visualization evaluation · User studies · Survey design · Policy analysis

Papers & projects

  • 2025 Evaluating novel visualization techniques for COEQWAL Co-author. Peer-reviewed paper, IEEE VIS 2025. A mixed-methods study with 57 participants measuring task performance, engagement, and cognitive load.
  • 2025 COEQWAL AI-enabled threshold analysis dashboard Compares AI-generated and manually curated policy analysis across water-resource outcome metrics. Code
