Papers

  • 2026 Predictive Modeling of High Energy Physics Workflows on Federated HPC Batch Systems A. Austin, L. Tang, J. Mueller, A. Norman, K.-L. Ma. Manuscript. A framework for predicting queue wait time, job failure, and fault attribution from 64 million Fermilab jobs across 46 sites, using only features known when the prediction is made and a chronological train/test split. Random splits overstated failure recall badly: an XGBoost model expected to catch 88.9% of failures caught only 66.2%. PDFCodeSlides
  • 2025 Watering the Future: Assessing a Visualization Design for Accessible Comprehension of Water Management Co-author. IEEE VIS 2025. Bottle-shaped "gradient glyphs" and droplet dotplots as an alternative to exceedance plots for California Central Valley water allocation data. In a 57-participant study, the glyphs matched exceedance plots on time-to-insight and scored significantly higher on novelty, attention, and discovery. PDF
